Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-26

QuoteEditor ("the App") is a small internal automation used to publish daily quote images to the "Quote of the Day" Facebook Page and Instagram account.

Data Collected

The App does not collect, store, or process personal data from end users. It only uses the access tokens and account identifiers of the Facebook Page and Instagram account it publishes to, on behalf of their owner.

Data Use

Any data accessed via the Facebook/Instagram Graph API (Page ID, Instagram Business account ID, and access tokens) is used solely to publish scheduled quote images to those accounts. No data is shared with third parties.

Data Retention

No end-user data is retained. Access tokens are stored securely as environment variables in the App's hosting provider and are not accessible to any third party.
